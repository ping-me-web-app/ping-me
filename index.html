Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, sticker durability, and how Ping Me works.

Does the person scanning my sticker need to install an app? +
No! Any smartphone camera (iPhone or Android) scans the QR code directly and opens an ultra-fast web interface in their mobile browser. They simply select why they are contacting you and click send.
How does Ping Me keep my phone number completely hidden? +
When someone scans your sticker, they interact exclusively with our secure proxy relay server. The message is encrypted and forwarded to your linked WhatsApp or SMS. Neither the person scanning nor anyone reading the QR data can see your real telephone number.
What prevents someone from repeatedly spamming me? +
Ping Me has built-in smart rate-limiting. A single device can only send 2 alerts within 15 minutes. In addition, our automated filter rejects abusive language and gives you an instant 1-tap "Block Sender" button.
Will the sticker withstand hot summers, car washes, and rain? +
Yes! Our stickers are printed on premium automotive-grade vinyl with an anti-UV laminate. When placed on the inside of your windshield, they are 100% protected from wiper friction, car wash brushes, and outdoor sun degradation.
